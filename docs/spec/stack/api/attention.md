# API: attention templates and numerical contracts

Clause format, ids and evidence tags: `../README.md`. The host and value-model
witnesses below do not establish silicon execution or measured throughput.
Numerical reports are not tolerance-based accuracy certificates. SR witnesses
check encoded streams and ownership, not SR numerical execution.

## 1. Template ownership and physical forms

- **API.ATTN.001** `unproven` Generated `flash`, `flashd` and `srflash` instantiate the hand streams by substituting ownership bounds, global origins and private scratch slots; inner images remain the template's MG/VC/SR algorithm. Each active share retains its prologue, epilogue and closing drains, including short shares. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash.rs:59-98, :100-134; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flashd.rs:12-57; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/srflash.rs:12-60; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176)
- **API.ATTN.002** `unproven` Sysnode assignments partition heads and complete query groups, not independently normalized key ranges. Each share reads every key of its heads. Head, query-row, heads-by-query and balanced flattened assignments use the ordered sysnode grant; empty shares issue only their join barrier. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention.rs:97-152; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:136-177, :240-249, :264-328; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176)
- **API.ATTN.003** `unproven` Generated motifs accept 1 through 10 heads and complete query groups up to 1024 rows per head: 128 rows for flash/flashd, 256 for srflash. Their prepacked interface fixes D=64 and 1024 keys; Q is already scaled by log2(e)/sqrt(64). Q/K and output retain a 1024-row head pitch for query prefixes. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:74-102; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:28-50, :200-204, :221-229; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176)
- **API.ATTN.004** `unproven` Physical Q/K packing is gm=32/gn=128 with nk=1 for flash and nk=2 for flashd/srflash. V uses gn=16,nk=16 for flash/flashd and gn=8,nk=32 for srflash. Output is GT4 tiled 8x16 for flash/flashd and 1x16 for srflash. Flash/flashd store each head's V contiguously; SR stores all heads' first 32 value columns, then all heads' second 32 columns, and takes an explicit f32[256,8] coefficient table `tab`. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:130-154; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:28-50, :282-299; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/srflash.rs:42-54; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176)
- **API.ATTN.005** `unproven` Private scratch per active node is S=1 MiB, O=64 KiB, li=16 KiB for flash; O=64 KiB, li=16 KiB for flashd; O=256 KiB for srflash. Scratch regions use logical-node rank, independently of the physical sysnode id. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:65-71; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:142-158, :318-326; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176)
- **API.ATTN.006** `unproven` A flashd ownership unit is 128 queries, including the score tile and four PV microtiles. Short shares retain the two-subrow score ring, eight-tile O/li rings, initial 256 score credits, returned 64-credit blocks, closing lookahead subrow and four closing sink drains; one-group shares still warm up and perform standalone tail combines. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flashd.rs:12-57; software/kohakutpu/language/kohakutpu/language/examples/l1/fused/flashd.ktpu:80-126, :170-187, :250-260, :265-279, :283-292; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:55-169 checks each output word's write count and zero remaining MG credits)
- **API.ATTN.007** `unproven` SR shares have at most two live heads, one MG pair and SR per head; longer head ranges run sequential pairs. Normalization owns complete 256-query groups with 512-row state reuse. Short shares normalize their final groups and return no unused PV-bank credits. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/srflash.rs:12-60; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:307-316; software/kohakutpu/language/kohakutpu/language/examples/l1/sru/srflash.ktpu:34-43, :45-57, :59-71; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:54-130, :133-176 checks encoded P=1 template equivalence, global write ownership and credit balance)
- **API.ATTN.008** `unproven` Capacity admission requires four distinct MG roles and either two VC roles for flash/flashd or two SR roles for srflash. Insufficient capacity receives DSL.KERN.020 before emission, rather than aliased live roles or another attention implementation. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:105-128; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:214-220; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_srflash.rs:133-176 witnesses the two-SR refusal; software/tests/stages/dsl/attention.rs:87-105 witnesses live-role capacity refusals)

## 2. Eager and tinygrad admission

- **API.ATTN.009** `unproven` Eager and tinygrad accept motif auto, flash or flashd and contract None or unit-scores. Tinygrad borrows realized device buffers and delegates selection to eager; srflash is a generated prepacked interface, not an eager packing route. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/api/tinygrad_attention.py:15-60; software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/kernels/attention.py:25-81; evidence: software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:17-61)
- **API.ATTN.010** `unproven` Auto without a bounded contract chooses sticky-overflow flash when eligible; auto with unit-scores chooses direct-ring flashd. Admission requires D=64, 1024 keys, 1..10 heads, whole 128-query groups through 1024 rows, four MG and two VC roles. An ineligible auto choice uses dense compatibility and records its refusal reason; an explicitly selected ineligible motif raises ValueError without replacement. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/kernels/attention.py:30-81; software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/api/attention.py:41-47; evidence: software/tests/runtime/test_attention.py:30-44, :47-65; software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:17-61)
- **API.ATTN.011** `unproven` Precise-scores requests never select an MX7 score motif. Auto retains the dense precise-scores path; an explicit flash/flashd request with precise_scores=True is ineligible and raises ValueError. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/kernels/attention.py:59-60; software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/api/attention.py:41-47, :86-107; evidence: software/tests/runtime/test_attention.py:68-80; software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:52-61 witnesses selector refusal)
- **API.ATTN.012** `unproven` Eager motif execution scales Q by log2(e)/8, packs Q/K with the selected motif's nk and V with nk=16, then copies only logical query rows from the 1024-row-pitch tiled output. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/api/attention.py:137-193; evidence: software/tests/runtime/test_attention.py:30-44; software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:17-61)
- **API.ATTN.013** `unproven` Successful generated motif launches record attention as the generator name and attention_reason as the admission reason. Dense compatibility launches record the refusal reason in attention, without a separate attention_reason field. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/api/attention.py:109-115, :169-175; evidence: software/tests/runtime/test_attention.py:30-80; software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:37-41)
- **API.ATTN.014** `unproven` Compiler admission uses API.OP.001 for invalid head/query bounds, unknown numerical contracts and flashd without unit-scores. Split legality records mx7/sticky-overflow, mx7/unit-scores or mx7/sr-row-max for the selected motif. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:81-102; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:233-239, :346-360; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:172-290; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:167-180)
- **API.ATTN.015** `unproven` Unit-scores declares that packed, scaled base-2 QK scores and packed V values are finite and in [-1,1]. Admission checks the declaration, not tensor elements; no tensor scan or device predicate enforces it. Absolute packed Q/K components at most 1/8 and packed V at most one suffice for the 64-product score bound. The direct-ring offset round(max(first 16 scores))+4 lies in [3,5], probabilities in [2^-6,2^-2], each 512-key half's sum in [8,128] and absolute partial output at most 128. These bounds prevent f16 partial overflow or vanishing sums; they do not certify f32 accuracy. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/kernels/attention.py:30-81; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:81-102; software/kohakutpu/language/kohakutpu/language/examples/l1/fused/flashd.ktpu:170-187; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210 and software/tests/runtime/test_attention_motifs.py:17-61 exercise bounded operands and report numerical statistics, not a proof over the domain)
- **API.ATTN.016** `unproven` Admission is O(1); instantiation and split pricing are compile-time work with no additional per-element predicate or data-path access. Each active share pays its own template prologue/epilogue. Split selection uses local timing estimates, not measured timings from this adapter. (code: ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/motif.rs:81-128; ktpu-kernels/src/gen/attention/flash_split.rs:330-371; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210 exercises split planning; cost established by implementation inspection)

## 3. Offline direct-ring execution

- **API.ATTN.017** `unproven` The value simulator executes encoded emit-to-unit GEMMs and credit-controlled drains through the same decoder for ENGINE fetch/mailbox entries and direct dispatch; textual L1 uses the same unit-output operations. Emission snapshots FP16 granules in sub-tile order; each burst of at most eight granules waits for and spends its entire credit amount, including the actual size of a final short burst. Fused DRAIN collects after the last handoff without transmitting the accumulator again; plain closing drains transmit resident sub-tiles and spend credits. These are value/ordering semantics of ISA.MG.026, .030, .035 and .050, not cycle-accurate throughput. (code: ktpu-sim/src/l0/mod.rs:201-249, :254-262; ktpu-sim/src/l0/words.rs:63-100; ktpu-sim/src/l1/node.rs:340-399; ktpu-sim/src/units/cluster.rs:124-134; ktpu-sim/src/machine.rs:47-57, :71-96, :258-285, :331-378; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210, :213-273)
- **API.ATTN.018** `unproven` VPOST shares the simulator drain queue but does not increment VDRAIN dispatch or completion counts. A drain-on-unpack sync therefore orders returned credits after its unpacks while marks/slack count drains, not posts (ISA.VC.140, .144, .153). (code: ktpu-sim/src/units/core/mod.rs:370-391, :493-543, :773-787; ktpu-sim/src/machine.rs:242-253; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:119-152)
- **API.ATTN.019** `unproven` COUNT advances the receiving VC's RX count by accepted granules; RX and MG credit balances survive RUN and package boundaries (ISA.VC.182). (code: ktpu-sim/src/units/core/mod.rs:216-223, :235-241; ktpu-sim/src/machine.rs:242-253, :274-283; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:119-152)
- **API.ATTN.020** `unproven` A credit-starved burst cannot complete its collecting mark; simulator execution returns the existing no-progress error without retry or replacement kernel. Queue-head and burst bookkeeping are constant-time apart from copying transmitted granules; output snapshots and copies are linear in payload size. (code: ktpu-sim/src/machine.rs:47-57, :71-96, :258-285; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:213-273 supplies seven credits to an eight-granule emission and requires the credit-wait error)
- **API.ATTN.021** `unproven` Direct-ring offline witnesses lower each invocation against the continuing resident tracker and execute on the continuing simulator, including repeats. They do not reset residency, credits or RX counts or replay a package specialized to a different entry state. Each share must spend its credits; each logical output word is written once per invocation and padding remains unwritten. P=1/2/4 reports compare against P=1 and f32, including max/mean/median absolute and relative errors, zero references and nonfinite counts; these reports do not define a tolerance-based pass. (code: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flash.rs:55-169; software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:102-210; evidence: software/tests/stages/l1_l0/attention_flashd.rs:142-210)
