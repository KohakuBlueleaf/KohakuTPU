# ISA: the matmul cluster

The matmul cluster (unit kind `MG`) as the stack drives it: its instruction
word, its operand L1, its accumulator, where its results go, its credits,
the tile shapes the compiler plans, and the quantiser step that makes its
operands. Number formats are on `numerics/formats.md`; this page names them.

Silicon evidence on this page is the v9t6 card's 15 `mm_` kernels and 25
`quant_` kernels (their statement tally: `gemm` 4471 statements, `drain`
314, `quant` 172, cluster `fill` with `n=` flags) and the LeNet training
run on v9t6 that matched CPU f32 (loss 2.3320 / 2.2305 against 2.3324 /
2.2289). On silicon the stack
used: memory FILLs into A0/A1/B0/B1 at entry 0, GEMMs of `nk` 1 and 2 with
and without `acc`, emitting GEMMs to memory, fused DRAINs to memory, two
clusters (`mg[0]`, `mg[1]`). Everything else here is at best RTL.

## The instruction word

- **ISA.MG.001** `unproven` Card runtime compares sorted enumerated MG, VC and SR coordinates against the board machine topology and raises CardMismatch on disagreement; enumeration does not silently replace a conflicting board list. Enumeration also rejects empty or differing mesh inventories. Unless an explicit machine is supplied, machine_of installs those coordinate lists and their lengths; its absent SIMT list becomes empty with count zero. Machine unit lookup indexes that kind list and returns no coordinate past its end. Card construction clones supplied unit lists into each mesh. (code: software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/runtime/card/__init__.py:202-228; software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/runtime/card/common.py:214-229; software/kohakutpu/application/kohakutpu/application/runtime/machine.py:112-127; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-machine/src/lib.rs:345-358; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-machine/src/card.rs:90-101; evidence: implementation inspection only; historical execution not established)
- **ISA.MG.002** `silicon` Cluster instructions use die.noc.data_bits [256] payload bits. Opcode [255:252] selects FILL one, GEMM two, DRAIN three or POST four. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:10-13, :63-65, :145-154; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:156, :278; evidence: documented original silicon scope:  the card-run tally `fn.gemm` 4471, `fn.drain` 314; additional named RTL witness: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v:248-275,946-966,1042-1046,1204-1209 exercises the four opcodes)
- **ISA.MG.003** `RTL` POST is opcode four. When gemm_busy is low, the sequencer presents the requested credit amount and destination and retires on acceptance. POST follows completion of the preceding sweep, not a specified fixed delay after its last operand read. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:896-897, :1011-1023; evidence: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v:958-966,1042-1071 checks amount, destination and sweep ordering)
- **ISA.MG.004** `unproven` Opcodes share field positions, not opcode-independent meanings. Layout: addr [251:218], n [217:202], sel [201], acc [200], gm [199:192], gn [191:184], nk [183:176], anchor [175:168], emit [116], fuse [115], abank [114], bbank [113], fbank [112], addr_hi [68:63]. In particular n counts FILL entries, DRAIN sub-tiles or POST credits. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:32-34, :100-134, :145-154; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:278-341; evidence: implementation inspection only; full historical or named-test coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.005** `unproven` Additional fields are peers [167:144], npeer [143:142], recv [141], eoff [140:133], aoff [132:125], boff [124:117], dnode [111], dst_x [110:107], dst_y [106:103], dbuf [102:95], dflags [94:87], dack_y [86:83], dack_x [82:79], dmesh [78:77], dfin [76:69] and credit [62]. Peer zero occupies the lowest peer coordinate bits. No decoded field in this RTL layout occupies [61:0]. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:113-134, :200-217; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:301-341; evidence: implementation inspection only; full historical or named-test coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.006** `unproven` The shared encoder format defaults nk to one, anchor to ClusterCfg.anchor, and all other non-opcode fields to zero. ClusterCfg::of sets anchor to ANCHOR [40]. Each format fixes its opcode. These are encoder defaults, not hardware requirements on unused fields. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:24-26, :63-72, :100-134, :145-154; evidence: implementation inspection only; full historical or named-test coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.007** `unproven` For die.noc.addr_bits [40], the encoder places address [33:0] in addr and [39:34] in addr_hi; RTL reconstructs their concatenation. The encoder rejects addresses exceeding the configured width. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:28-30, :63-66, :169-186; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:279-281; evidence: implementation inspection only; full historical or named-test coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.008** `unproven` A coordinate is encoded as `(y & mask(die.noc.pos_bits)) << die.noc.pos_bits | (x & mask(die.noc.pos_bits))`; `die.noc.pos_bits` [4] makes this a {y,x} byte. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:189-197; evidence: no named RTL test established for this clause)
- **ISA.MG.009** `unproven` The sequencer accepts instructions while idle and processes each opcode before returning idle. This does not serialize all effects to completion: GEMM retires from the sequencer after issuing its sweep, so subsequent instructions can proceed while the sweep remains active. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:895-919, :954-985, :1025-1028; evidence: implementation inspection only; full historical or named-test coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.094** `RTL` GEMM carries aoff/boff in fields [132:125]/[124:117]. RTL forwards them independently of operand bank selection. The pumped manager's eight-bit offset arithmetic wraps modulo 256 within each selected bank; zero offsets are not required. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:35, :117-118, :436-454; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:307-308, :902-903, :458-461; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump.v:159-160; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:221-226, :298-299; evidence: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump_tb.v:68-84,118-119,278-325,381-399 with MX_L1OFF/MX_L1WRAP)
- **ISA.MG.095** `unproven` The v9t6 top supplies unit.inst_depth [512] to both cluster instances. The endpoint instruction FIFO uses FIFO_DEPTH(INST_DEPTH) and DATA_WIDTH(FLIT_WIDTH). This is queue capacity, not a limit on all in-flight effects. Free space is INST_DEPTH minus inst_used, updated on accepted instruction flits and FIFO pops. (code: src/kohakutpu/top/generated/ktpu_ship_1x2_2c2v_1m_nol2_pump.v:77, :494-508; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:248-254; src/kohakuaccel/noc/endpoint/noc_cu_base.v:133-139, :155-169; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution not established)
- **ISA.MG.096** `unproven` DRAIN shares anchor [175:168], decoded and captured by RTL but not applied to DRAIN. Anchor feeds GEMM; drain start uses count, fused flag and send selection. Plain drain issues EMIT/SEND; fused drain waits for the requested results of the emitting GEMM, including results still to be produced. Typed DRAIN supplies no anchor override and uses default forty. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:26, :71, :112, :145-154, :456-460; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:287, :899, :458-464, :994-997; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump.v:410-437; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution not established)

Normal splitting places low 34 bits in addr and remaining six in addr_hi. FILL, DRAIN and emitting GEMM invoke this check; a non-emitting GEMM does not validate its stored address through this path. ## FILL

- **ISA.MG.010** `unproven` FILL normalizes encoded n=0 to one. For effective memory-FILL counts 1..255, its descriptor requests consecutive entries from addr with eoff in the transaction tag. The descriptor carries only the low eight bits of the effective count; raw larger counts do not request that many entries. Each entry comprises four 32-byte words. The receiving instruction selects side and bank. Assembly consumes thirty-two seven-bit values per word, lane-major for A and K-major for B, and four scales from word zero, committing the 928-bit entry on word three. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:535-546, :771-776, :809-855, :897, :932-950; src/kohakuaccel/sysnode/core/mag_mem_port.v:288-299, :393-399; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:897; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.011** `unproven` L2 splits a run above mg.max_fill [255] and no larger than twice that bound into ceil(n/2) and floor(n/2) entries, advancing the byte address and initial entry offset after each part. The current bank-capacity check rejects counts above mg.l1_bank_entries [256] before splitting, so the 256-entry case produces two 128-entry FILLs; the second offset is the initial offset plus 128. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster/fill.rs:69-77, :159-190; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.012** `unproven` For entry i the memory service returns tag (eoff+i) modulo 256 and a two-bit word index. The cluster places it at the concatenation of fbank and that tag. Its completed-entry counter determines completion, not placement. (code: src/kohakuaccel/sysnode/core/mag_mem_port.v:1268-1287, :1307-1317; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:348, :775-776, :940-950; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs:257-259; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.013** `unproven` A recv FILL sends no memory read. Its per-side pending-entry counter is ten bits and wraps modulo 1024. It compares against and subtracts the low ten bits of the effective instruction count (encoded zero first becomes one). Thus raw n=1024 compares against and subtracts zero, while 1024 unconsumed entry completions wrap the counter to zero. Those entries are placed using the CU_DATA buffer and offset, not the recv instruction bank or entry offset. The instruction address is not used for a memory request. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:774-791, :933-950; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:897; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.014** `unproven` A shared FILL names up to mg.max_peers [3] additional destinations. The lowest packed {y,x} member issues the read. The memory port sends each captured entry to the requester then each peer, replaying buffered words without another AXI read. L2 sends eligible private-fill prefixes to followers, marks them, queues their remaining steps including shared FILL, waits through those marks, then sends the leader step. A mark precedes the shared FILL and does not certify its completion. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:507-515, :928-938; src/kohakuaccel/sysnode/core/mag_mem_port.v:304-305, :406-417, :1291-1315; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/lower/share.rs:150-175; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.015** `unproven` Shared A FILL permits count 128 subject to ordinary view, bank-capacity and allocation checks; the shared-B limit does not apply to A. The shared-read descriptor represents this count, the leader issues the read, and FILL completion waits for 128 complete returned entries. This compiler/control support is not an empirical guarantee of end-to-end numerical correctness. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster/fill.rs:59-89, :160-189; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:512-515, :535-546, :932-950; evidence: implementation inspection only; named shared-A128 numerical assertion not established)
- **ISA.MG.016** `unproven` Memory responses and CU_DATA into operand L1 share entry assembly. Hardware does not make arbitrary overlapping streams safe. The normal verifier records L1 bursts and non-recv FILL response windows and reports a Port finding when a burst may land during responses, based on causal send/landing and fill-start/end events. Its explicit cluster checker mutant suppresses this check. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:760-776, :809-855; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs:71-75, :224-233, :417-426; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-verify/src/sim/report.rs:116, :169-215; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.017** `unproven` The memory FILL descriptor selects no transform. Cluster assembly interprets each four-word entry with fixed MXFP7 packing rather than choosing a format from the data or instruction. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:535-546, :809-846; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.018** `unproven` A FILL's `n` is at most `mg.max_fill` [255]; encoding returns a refusal under ISA.MG.018 for a larger count and under RUL.048 for `eoff + n > mg.l1_bank_entries` [256]. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:391-411; evidence: no card or RTL test established for these refusal paths)
- **ISA.MG.019** `unproven` The memory service truncates eoff+i to its eight-bit response tag. The receiving cluster prepends fbank independently, so raw response offsets wrap modulo mg.l1_bank_entries [256] without carrying into the other bank. Normal encoder bank bounds prevent requesting this wrap; this clause describes raw hardware behavior, not a tested crossing. (code: src/kohakuaccel/sysnode/core/mag_mem_port.v:367-369, :399, :1312; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:775; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs:257-259; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:401-411; evidence: implementation inspection only; qualifying execution coverage not established)
- **ISA.MG.098** `unproven` L2 refuses explicit shared B fills whose computed entry count exceeds mg.shared_b_max [64], with L2.MG.006. Target reads this machine bound; automatic sharing leaves annotated B fills unshared above it. This is a compiler limit, not a hardware size validator. (code: machines/multimesh_v9t6.json:189; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/target.rs:113-119; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster/fill.rs:59-89; software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-l2/src/passes/share.rs:35-57; evidence: host implementation inspection only)

## GEMM

- **ISA.MG.020** `silicon` The pumped manager traverses K-pair starts outermost, then row group g and column group h. Each issue targets sub-tile g*gn+h and reads A at (aoff+g*nk+kb) modulo mg.l1_bank_entries [256], B at (boff+h*nk+kb) modulo that bound, in the selected banks. Phase two reads the next entry within each bank. kb advances by two; a lone final block is marked single. Each sub-tile first K-pair issue loads if acc is zero; its later K-pair issues add. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:139-142, :221-244, :327-344; evidence: documented original silicon scope:  `mm_` kernels, e.g. `gemm 38x8x2 ... acc=1` chains in `mm_af3e686e21`; additional named RTL witness: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump_tb.v:270-340,346-401,457-499, with offset/wrap configurations at :68-79)
- **ISA.MG.021** `silicon` abank and bbank select operand banks; aoff and boff select their initial entry offsets. Zero offset is not a hardware requirement. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:221-226, :298-304; evidence: documented original silicon scope:  silicon sweeps alternate A0/A1, B0/B1 at offset 0; additional named RTL witness: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump_tb.v:68-79,118-119,270-340,346-401; tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v:513-663,1172-1175)
- **ISA.MG.022** `unproven` The typed encoder accepts nk equal to one or a multiple of mg.kstep [2], refusing other values under ISA.MG.099. This host restriction differs from the current manager handling of an unpaired final K block. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:221-222, :425-434; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:244, :332; evidence: implementation inspection only; no qualifying execution established for the complete contract)
- **ISA.MG.023** `unproven` The default GEMM anchor is forty, but the typed encoder carries a supplied anchor. The pumped accumulator multiplies each partial magnitude by its own E5M3 mantissa product with exponent ea+eb-anchor-6. It aligns and sign-aware merges the two partials before normalization; magnitude overflow raises the merged exponent. LOAD adds the normalized merged value to zero, while ADD adds it to resident state. (code: software/kohakutpu/compiler/crates/ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs:24-26, :306-320, :445; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_acu_fp_pump.v:219-229, :253-288, :339-382, :404-408, :463, :528, :566, :578-587, :616-625; evidence: implementation inspection only; no qualifying execution established for the complete contract)
- **ISA.MG.024** `unproven` GEMM retires from the sequencer after issuing its sweep, so later instructions may proceed while it remains active. For effective nonzero dimensions the pumped issue count is gm*gn*ceil(nk/2), with additional pacing, stalls and pipeline completion time. gm*gn*nk is not a cycle-count contract. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:975-985; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:238-271, :305-341; evidence: implementation inspection only; no qualifying execution established for the complete contract)
- **ISA.MG.025** `unproven` For `nk > 1`, the pumped manager inserts four idle cycles after each issue for one sub-tile, two for two sub-tiles, and one for three or four sub-tiles; it inserts none for at least five sub-tiles or `nk <= 1`. Zero `gm` and `gn` fields each mean one. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:196-206, :305-334; evidence: no named test of this manager's pacing established)
- **ISA.MG.026** `silicon` An emitting GEMM marks each sub-tile final K-pair issue. The manager selects ADD_EMIT, or LOAD with the separate load-and-emit flag if the issue opens the tile. The accumulator writes the rounded result and emits its FP16 conversion into the drain FIFO. This enqueue is distinct from external memory completion. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:327-333, :425-434; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_acu_fp_pump.v:718-742, :947-990; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump.v:305-318, :332-334; evidence: documented original silicon scope:  every silicon `mm_` tile drains this way; additional named RTL witness: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v:403-510,1159-1169 with MX_CU_PUMP checks emitting output)
- **ISA.MG.027** `unproven` Emitting GEMM waits for drain_busy to clear as well as sweep/peer exclusion. Issue stalls at d_out >= DQ_DEPTH-16 [4080 for mg.dq_depth=4096]. d_out counts granules reserved by issued commands and not yet taken, including in-flight results and queued data. Fused emit reserves one per emit_issue; plain EMIT reserves one and SEND two; each accepted drain granule subtracts one. drain_busy includes drain sequencing, outstanding reservations and drain wait state, not external write completion. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:975-981; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump.v:252-261, :293-318, :335-341, :383-405, :434-445; evidence: implementation inspection only; no qualifying execution established for the complete contract)
- **ISA.MG.028** `silicon` With nk one or two, acc zero and emit set, each sub-tile completes in one pumped issue, not necessarily the whole sweep. OP_LOAD with le=1 is forwarded to the accumulator, which performs zero-plus-chain, writes the rounded loaded value and emits its FP16 conversion into the drain queue. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:327-332, :425-450; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump.v:507-527, :305-318; src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_acu_fp_pump.v:616-617, :708-742, :947-990; evidence: documented original silicon scope:  `mm_6a1f44575f`, `gemm 30x8x2 a=A0 b=B0 acc=0 emit=h1+0`; additional named RTL witness: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v:403-510,1159-1169 with MX_CU_PUMP checks nk=1 and nk=2 emission)
- **ISA.MG.029** `RTL` Operand entry arithmetic wraps within the 256-entry bank independently of bank selection, including the second-phase increment. (code: src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:139-142, :221-226; tests/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump_tb.v:68-79; evidence: tests/matmul/mx_cluster_node_pump_tb.v:68-79,270-340,346-401 with MX_L1OFF and MX_L1WRAP)
- **ISA.MG.099** `unproven` With mg.kstep [2], the typed encoder permits nk=1 or even nk and rejects odd nk>=3. The pumped manager uses widened final-pair arithmetic and marks a lone final block.
