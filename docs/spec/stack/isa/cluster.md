# ISA: the matmul cluster

The matmul cluster (unit kind `MG`) as the stack drives it: its instruction
word, its operand L1, its accumulator, where its results go, its credits,
the tile shapes the compiler plans, and the quantiser step that makes its
operands. Number formats are on `numerics/formats.md`; this page names them.

Silicon evidence on this page is the v9t6 card's 15 `mm_` kernels and 25
`quant_` kernels (their statement tally: `gemm` 4471 statements, `drain`
314, `quant` 172, cluster `fill` with `n=` flags) and the LeNet training
run on v9t6 that matched CPU f32 (loss 2.3320 / 2.2305 against 2.3324 /
2.2289). On silicon the stack
used: memory FILLs into A0/A1/B0/B1 at entry 0, GEMMs of `nk` 1 and 2 with
and without `acc`, emitting GEMMs to memory, fused DRAINs to memory, two
clusters (`mg[0]`, `mg[1]`). Everything else here is at best RTL.

## The instruction word

- **ISA.MG.001** `silicon` The stack reads the cluster count and coordinates from the machine (`mg.count` [2 on v9t6]); on the card the enumerated units replace the board's list. Units are addressed `mg[J]`, J in 0..`mg.count`-1. (code: `ktpu-machine/src/card.rs`; `software/application/kohakutpu/application/runtime/card.py:166-167`; evidence: v9t6 `mm_` kernels send to `mg[0]` and `mg[1]` only)
- **ISA.MG.002** `silicon` A cluster instruction is one `die.noc.data_bits` [256] payload with the opcode in [255:252]: FILL 1, GEMM 2, DRAIN 3. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `src/kohakutpu/matmul/mx_cluster_cu.v:23,156`; doc: `docs/projects/kohakutpu/isa.md:75-85`; evidence: the card-run tally `fn.gemm` 4471, `fn.drain` 314)
- **ISA.MG.003** `RTL` POST is opcode 4: once no earlier sweep still reads L1 (`gemm_busy` low), the cluster sends a CREDIT of `n` (32-byte granules) to (`dst_x`, `dst_y`). (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:448,455-459`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`, POST leaving 56 ns after the sweep's last L1 read)
- **ISA.MG.004** `silicon` Every opcode shares one field layout; no field's meaning depends on the opcode. Fields the card decoded on silicon: `addr` [251:218], `n` [217:202], `sel` [201], `acc` [200], `gm` [199:192], `gn` [191:184], `nk` [183:176], `anchor` [175:168], `emit` [116], `fuse` [115], `abank` [114], `bbank` [113], `fbank` [112], `addr_hi` [68:63]. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:21-69,279-341`; doc: `isa.md:88-94`; evidence: the 15 `mm_` kernels)
- **ISA.MG.005** `RTL` The remaining fields: `peers` [167:144] (up to three {y,x} bytes, peer 0 low), `npeer` [143:142], `recv` [141], `eoff` [140:133], `dnode` [111], `dst_x` [110:107], `dst_y` [106:103], `dbuf` [102:95], `dflags` [94:87], `dack_y` [86:83], `dack_x` [82:79], `dmesh` [78:77], `dfin` [76:69], `credit` [62]; [61:0] is zero. `aoff` and `boff` are ISA.MG.094. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:36-61`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v` (recv, dnode, dbuf, dflags, dack, credit); `software/application/tests/test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes` (peers; `eoff` 128 on the second half of each 256-entry A fill, `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`), `::test_split_k_across_meshes` (dmesh, dfin))
- **ISA.MG.006** `silicon` Fields an instruction does not use carry their defaults: `nk` 1, `anchor` 40 (the stack's anchor, `2 * SBIAS`), every other field 0. That a DRAIN ignores `anchor` is ISA.MG.096. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:228-230`; `number-format.md:192-195`; evidence: every silicon FILL / DRAIN payload)
- **ISA.MG.007** `silicon` An address is `die.noc.addr_bits` [40] bits: bits [33:0] in `addr`, bits [39:34] in `addr_hi`; the cluster decodes `{addr_hi, addr}`. The encoder's refusal of a wider address is ISA.MG.097. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:279-281`; evidence: every silicon FILL / emitting GEMM / DRAIN address)
- **ISA.MG.008** `RTL` A unit coordinate in a field is the byte `(y & 0xF) << 4 | (x & 0xF)`. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes` (`peers`), `::test_a_drain_into_the_node` (`dst_x`, `dst_y`); every silicon cluster instruction left these fields 0)
- **ISA.MG.009** `RTL` The node sends a cluster's words in program order; the cluster executes them in order, one at a time. The FIFO depth is ISA.MG.095. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_matmul_four_clusters` on `vlt_card_v9_1n_v2`)
- **ISA.MG.094** `unproven` `aoff` [132:125] and `boff` [124:117] carry a GEMM's entry offsets (ISA.MG.029). (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:36-61`; evidence: none; no named test was verified to emit a nonzero `aoff` or `boff`)
- **ISA.MG.095** `unproven` A cluster's instruction FIFO holds `unit.inst_depth` [512] words in flight. (code: `ktpu-machine/src/card.rs`; evidence: none; no test fills the FIFO)
- **ISA.MG.096** `unproven` A DRAIN's `anchor` is decoded and ignored. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; doc: `number-format.md:192-195`; evidence: none; every DRAIN carries the default anchor)
- **ISA.MG.097** `unproven` An address that does not fit 40 bits is refused at encode. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; evidence: none; a software refusal no card or RTL run takes)

## FILL

- **ISA.MG.010** `silicon` FILL streams `n` L1 entries from byte address `addr` into side `sel` (0 = A, 1 = B), bank `fbank`, entries `eoff`.. of that bank. One entry is 128 bytes in memory (four 32-byte words: the MXFP7 entry of `numerics/formats.md`). (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:24-28,40,49,67-69`; doc: `isa.md:128-138`; evidence: silicon fills `n=8`..`n=76` (the card-run tally `fn:fill:flag`) into A0, A1, B0, B1 at entry 0)
- **ISA.MG.011** `RTL` The L2 lowering splits a run of 256..510 entries into two halves, the second at `eoff` = the first's length. The limit and the other cases are ISA.MG.018. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes` (each `fill x[0 : +256, k*128 : +128]` is 256 entries: two FILLs of 128, the second `at=128`))
- **ISA.MG.012** `RTL` A FILL's entries land at `{fbank, eoff + i}`, i < n. The wrap past the bank's end is ISA.MG.019. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:217-226`; doc: `isa.md:283-303`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes` (`eoff` 0 and 128, n 128); `::test_matmul_four_clusters` (`eoff` 0))
- **ISA.MG.013** `RTL` FILL with `recv` 1 sends no memory request: it completes once `n` entries have landed on side `sel` by CU_DATA bursts flagged COUNT and takes them; entries land into a counter per side and the FILL subtracts its `n`. Its `addr` is 0. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:446,450-453`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`, a recv FILL issued 2,000 cycles before its operands)
- **ISA.MG.014** `RTL` A shared FILL names up to `mg.max_peers` [3] sharers in `peers`/`npeer`; the lowest {y,x} of the set issues the memory read and the others receive the same entries; a follower must already be executing that FILL when the entries come, which the node orders with mark / wait. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes`)
- **ISA.MG.015** `RTL` Shared A FILLs of 128 entries are right. The shared B limit is ISA.MG.098. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_shared_fills_on_two_nodes` (`share=cross` A fills, 128 entries each))
- **ISA.MG.016** `RTL` FILL responses and CU_DATA bursts into L1 share one entry-assembly register: a memory FILL's response window and a CU_DATA burst into the same cluster never overlap; the verifier reports an overlap. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/sync.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers` (cores stream into a cluster that also fills from memory))
- **ISA.MG.017** `unproven` A FILL carries no format bit: every FILL reads four 32-byte words an entry and takes them as MXFP7; memory holds no map of formats. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs` (no format field); doc: `isa.md:152-157`; `number-format.md:262-267`; evidence: silicon fills read MXFP7 the mover made, which shows the reading, not the absence of other formats)
- **ISA.MG.018** `unproven` A FILL's `n` is at most `mg.max_fill` [255]; the encoder raises ValueError above it. The L2 lowering issues a run of at most 255 whole and a run above 510 as 255 and the rest. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: none; no named test fills 255 or more than 510 entries in one run, and a refusal is a software path)
- **ISA.MG.019** `unproven` A FILL's entry offset wraps inside its bank (`(eoff + i) mod mg.l1_bank_entries`) and never carries into the other bank. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:217-226`; doc: `isa.md:283-303`; evidence: none; no named test fills past a bank's end)
- **ISA.MG.098** `unproven` A shared B FILL holds at most `mg.shared_b_max` [64] entries; the L2 lowering refuses a larger one. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: none named; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs` records a Verilated-card measurement (64 right, 96 and 128 wrong) that no test keeps)

## GEMM

- **ISA.MG.020** `silicon` GEMM sweeps `gm` x `gn` output sub-tiles over `nk` K blocks with K outermost: for kb, for g < gm, for h < gn, sub-tile `g*gn + h` takes `L1A[aoff + g*nk + kb] x L1B[boff + h*nk + kb]`; the first K block LOADs unless `acc` is 1, every other block ADDs. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:221-226`; doc: `isa.md:178-218`; evidence: `mm_` kernels, e.g. `gemm 38x8x2 ... acc=1` chains in `mm_af3e686e21`)
- **ISA.MG.021** `silicon` `abank`/`bbank` pick the L1 bank a sweep reads on each side, reading from entry offset 0. Nonzero `aoff`/`boff` are ISA.MG.029. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:217-226`; doc: `isa.md:267-303`; evidence: silicon sweeps alternate A0/A1, B0/B1 at offset 0)
- **ISA.MG.022** `silicon` The stack emits `nk` 1 or a multiple of `mg.kstep` [2]; sweeps of `nk` 1 and 2 ran. Odd `nk` ≥ 3 is ISA.MG.099. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:98,133`; evidence: silicon sweeps of `nk` 1 and 2)
- **ISA.MG.023** `silicon` `anchor` is 40 (the stack's anchor, `2 * SBIAS`) on every GEMM the stack emits; the accumulator applies `exp = ea + eb - anchor - 6` to each K block's integer partial. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_acu_fp.v:133-159`; doc: `number-format.md:167-190`; evidence: `mm_` kernels)
- **ISA.MG.024** `silicon` A GEMM retires from the sequencer when its sweep starts; the sweep runs on for about `gm*gn*nk` cycles. The next FILL may start while it runs. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:244-265`; evidence: `mm_` kernels fill the other bank while the previous sweep runs)
- **ISA.MG.025** `RTL` Two commands to one accumulator address are at least `mg.reuse_min` [5] cycles apart; the manager inserts idle cycles when `gm*gn` < 5 (4 at 1, 2 at 2, 1 at 3 or 4); the stack does not pace. (RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:382`; doc: `isa.md:232-242`; `accumulator.md:218-235`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_acu_fp_tb.v`)
- **ISA.MG.026** `silicon` A GEMM with `emit` 1 hands each sub-tile out as its last K block completes it, to `addr` (memory); a GEMM without `emit` carries `addr` 0. Emitting to a unit (`dnode`) is ISA.MG.033. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:43-44`; doc: `isa.md:313-341`; evidence: every silicon `mm_` tile drains this way)
- **ISA.MG.027** `RTL` An emitting sweep starts only once everything queued before it has left the drain queue; a sweep is held while more than `mg.dq_depth` - 16 [4080] sub-tiles are outstanding. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:96`; doc: `isa.md:328-335`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_matmul_four_clusters`)
- **ISA.MG.028** `silicon` A sweep of one issue (`nk` ≤ `mg.kstep`) with `acc` 0 may emit (load-and-emit). (doc: `isa.md:337-341`; evidence: `mm_6a1f44575f`, `gemm 30x8x2 a=A0 b=B0 acc=0 emit=h1+0`)
- **ISA.MG.029** `unproven` `aoff`/`boff` are 8-bit entry offsets inside the bank a sweep reads and wrap inside it. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:217-226`; doc: `isa.md:267-303`; evidence: none; the hand-written L1 examples sweep at offset 0 and no named L2 test was verified to emit a nonzero offset)
- **ISA.MG.099** `unproven` An odd `nk` ≥ 3 corrupts sub-tile (0, 0) on the pumped manager, so the encoder and the L2 lowering refuse it. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:98,133`; evidence: none named; the failure is a measurement on `card_v9_1n` recorded at `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`)

## DRAIN

- **ISA.MG.030** `silicon` DRAIN with `fuse` 1 issues no reads: it waits until the sub-tiles of the emitting GEMM it collects have left, holding the sequencer. Its `addr`, `n` and sink equal that GEMM's (`n = gm*gn`); every emitting GEMM is collected by exactly one fused DRAIN, in order. (code: `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:45-46`; doc: `isa.md:322-326`; evidence: the card-run tally `fn:drain:flag`, n = 24..672, every silicon drain fused)
- **ISA.MG.031** `silicon` A drain to memory writes one 32-byte word a 4x4 sub-tile, in sweep order, sub-tile t at `addr + 32 t`, element (i, j) at FP16 `4i + j` of the word. Values are FP16 by NUM.ACC.004. (code: `ktpu-l1/src/layout/tensor.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/memory.rs`; doc: `isa.md:309-311`; evidence: `mm_` kernels' `h1` tiles read by the cores' `gt4` unpack)
- **ISA.MG.032** `RTL` DRAIN with `fuse` 0 (plain) reads `n` resident sub-tiles once the array is idle and retires when its last write has left the unit. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:352-356`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_split_k_through_the_peer_accumulator`, `::test_a_drain_into_the_node`)
- **ISA.MG.033** `RTL` A drain (or emitting GEMM) to a unit (`dnode` 1) sends CU_DATA bursts to (`dst_x`, `dst_y`) buffer `dbuf` from granule `addr / 32`; `dflags` bit 0 (SIGNAL) asks the sink for DATA_RECEIVED to (`dack_x`, `dack_y`), (0,0) meaning back to the sender; bit 1 (COUNT) makes the sink count the burst. `count` and `credit` are refused on a drain to memory. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:362-414`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`; `test_l1_examples.py::test_a_drain_into_the_node`)
- **ISA.MG.034** `RTL` A drain to another mesh sets `dmesh` (0..3) and `dfin` (the final {y,x} there, nonzero); `dst` then names this mesh's memory port. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:57,61-65`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_split_k_across_meshes`)
- **ISA.MG.035** `RTL` Drain bursts are at most `mg.wburst` [8] sub-tiles and close when the queue runs dry. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:1047`; doc: `isa.md:343-356`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_matmul_four_clusters`)

## CU_DATA buffers

- **ISA.MG.040** `RTL` A cluster takes CU_DATA into three buffers: 0 L1 A and 1 L1 B (a granule is one of an entry's four words; `mg.l1_a_entries` / `mg.l1_b_entries` x 4 [2048] granules each), 2 the accumulator through ADD_PEER (a sub-tile is two granules, `mg.tiles` x 2 [8192] granules, the offset even). Buffers 0/1 take exactly a memory response's bytes. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:161,643`; doc: `isa.md:373-393`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`)
- **ISA.MG.041** `RTL` A burst past its buffer, into an unknown buffer, or into buffer 2 at an odd granule is rejected: its data flits are consumed and dropped, and it is still acknowledged. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:643`; doc: `isa.md:395-414`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`)
- **ISA.MG.042** `RTL` A receiver holds one open stream: two senders never interleave bursts into one cluster; the node orders them. (doc: `isa.md:416-420`; code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs` (landings recorded for the order check); evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`)
- **ISA.MG.043** `RTL` A drain into another cluster's accumulator (`drain c -> mg[J] add`, buffer 2) adds at accumulator precision (the FP22 sub-tile, two granules); the receiving tile is already open, no sweep runs on it during the stream, and `mg.reuse_min` holds within the stream. A tile another cluster adds into is drained plain, never fused. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; doc: `accumulator.md:310-345`; `isa.md:422-433`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_split_k_through_the_peer_accumulator`, `::test_l2_split_k`)
- **ISA.MG.044** `unproven` The L2 lowering keeps one cross-node accumulator add in flight at a time (two at once hang). (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/mod.rs`; evidence: none names it; the hand-written cross-mesh add `test_l1_examples.py::test_split_k_across_meshes` passes)
- **ISA.MG.045** `unproven` A self-addressed peer burst deadlocks (the receive path cannot add until the send drain retires). No stack check refuses `drain c -> mg[J] add` with J the draining cluster itself. (doc: `isa.md:428-433`; code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs` (no such check); evidence: none; `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v` avoids it by replaying the burst after the drain)
- **ISA.MG.046** `RTL` After an accumulator add the adding cluster sends a marker, `drain n=4 to=mg[J] buf=0 l1=0 count` (one entry into J's A0 at entry 0); cluster J takes the markers with `fill A0 recv n=<adds>` before its next segment, so it drains only after every add landed. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/mod.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_split_k` (same node); the cross-node form has no L2 test)

## Credits and streams

- **ISA.MG.050** `RTL` With `credit` 1, each burst of a drain or emitting GEMM to a unit leaves only once the cluster holds its length in credits, and spends them; credits come from the sink's POST / VPOST. A credit is one 32-byte granule. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; doc: `isa.md:447`; evidence: `tests/matmul/mx_cluster_data_tb.v`: a 16-sub-tile credit drain sends nothing before a credit, one 9-flit burst per 8 credits after)
- **ISA.MG.051** `RTL` A streamed operand (`a = recv vc[H] : mx7a[M, K] @A` in a loop) is a `fill ... recv` of `M/4 x K/32` entries, a whole divisor of a bank; a stage receives a stream at least twice; the first recv posts the sender the ring's granules in POST words of at most 512; each later recv's room goes back after the value's last use, except the last two. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`: the ring is two receiver slots, at most both banks (512 entries), and a POST word carries 512 credits; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers`)
- **ISA.MG.052** `RTL` A POST is a CU_SIGNAL inside the cluster's own mesh; the stack refuses a POST to another mesh. (code: `ktpu-l1/src/lower/node/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers`)
- **ISA.MG.053** `RTL` At the end of a program every entry landed by COUNT has been taken by a `recv` FILL and every credit received has been spent; the verifier reports what is left. (code: `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py` stream tests)

## Operand L1

- **ISA.MG.060** `silicon` Each side (A, B) holds `mg.l1_a_entries` / `mg.l1_b_entries` [512] entries as two banks of `mg.l1_bank_entries` [256]; the bank bit is the ninth bit of the entry index. A side's values live in one bank. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/cluster.rs`; RTL top2 `:69-70`; `mx_cluster_mgr_pump.v:229-232`; doc: `isa.md:283-303`; evidence: silicon fills and sweeps on A0/A1/B0/B1)
- **ISA.MG.061** `silicon` An entry is 4 lanes x `mg.kblock` [32] K values: 128 int7 significands and four E5M3 scales, `mg.entry_bits` [928] bits as held, 128 bytes in memory. An A value `mx7a[M, K]` occupies `(M/4) x (K/32)` entries, lane group g's K block kb at entry `g*nk + kb`. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l1/src/layout/tensor.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/memory.rs`; doc: `matmul.md:297-303`; `number-format.md:229-234`; evidence: `mm_` kernels)
- **ISA.MG.062** `silicon` A value on side A is `mx7a`, on side B `mx7b` (the B packing); rows are whole groups of 4 and K whole blocks of 32; one value's entries fit one bank (`(rows/4) * nk` ≤ `mg.l1_bank_entries`). (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `software/language/kohakutpu/language/dsl/ops.py:216`; evidence: `mm_` kernels: `mx7a[.., ..] @dram(gm=.., nk=..)`, `mx7b[..] @dram(gn=.., nk=..)`)
- **ISA.MG.063** `RTL` A fill takes the bank of its side that holds no value still read and that the latest sweep does not read, alternating with the side's previous bank; with no such bank the lowering refuses. A sweep's bank stays read until the node learns the array is idle (POST, DRAIN, or a fused DRAIN holding the sequencer). (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_matmul_four_clusters`; silicon programs alternate banks this way)
- **ISA.MG.064** `unproven` A sweep may read rows `LO .. LO+R` of an operand value (`X[LO : +R, :]`, made by the `split` pass when a bank is too small for M or N at K) with LO and R whole groups of 4: the same bank, `LO/4 * nk` entries further in. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `ktpu-l2/src/passes/split.rs`; evidence: no named test reaches it)

## Accumulator

- **ISA.MG.070** `silicon` The accumulator holds 4x4 sub-tiles; an `@acc` value `f32[M, N]` is whole sub-tiles with `(M/4)(N/4)` ≤ `mg.tiles`, sub-tile `g*gn + h`. A value of all `mg.tiles` [4096] is ISA.MG.074. The stack's type for it says `f32`; the hardware holds FP22 (NUM.ACC.001). (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; `kernels/matmul.py:19,62-63`; RTL top2 `:54`; doc: `accumulator.md:76-110`; evidence: silicon tiles up to 21 x 32 = 672 sub-tiles)
- **ISA.MG.071** `silicon` A sub-tile holds 16 values of `mg.acc_mantissa_bits + 8` [22] bits (S1 E7 M14, bias 63). (RTL `mx_cluster_cu.v:83,163`; `mx_fpacc.v:1-12`; doc: `accumulator.md:76,113-121`; evidence: every silicon GEMM; format clauses NUM.ACC.*)
- **ISA.MG.072** `silicon` `zero` opens a tile without an instruction: the next GEMM into it has `acc` 0; a GEMM into a swept tile has `acc` 1. A K longer than one fill chains GEMMs into one tile. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: `mm_f96c521ab7`: `acc=0` then `acc=1`, `acc=1`)
- **ISA.MG.073** `unproven` The tile memory has no reset: a tile is read only after a LOAD opened it. (doc: `isa.md:422-425`; code: none checks it beyond ISA.MG.072)
- **ISA.MG.074** `RTL` A sweep of `gm * gn` = `mg.tiles` [4096] sub-tiles uses the whole accumulator. (RTL top2 `:54`; code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_matmul_four_clusters` (`gemm 64x64x2`, 4096 sub-tiles a tile))

## Tile shapes the compiler uses

- **ISA.MG.080** `silicon` A contraction `[M, K] x [N, K]` is planned as: N whole 32-column blocks (else refused); K padded to `mg.kblock` multiples (Kp); `nk` = 2 when Kp is a multiple of 64, else 1 (K chunk 32 `nk`); M padded to the row quantum (4, or 16 when the epilogue reads a statistic); `tm` the largest multiple of 4 ≤ 256 dividing Mp, a multiple of the quantum, with `(tm/4) nk` ≤ `mg.l1_bank_entries`; `tn` the widest of 256/128/64/32 dividing N, halved while `(tm/4)(tn/4)` > `mg.tiles`. (code: `software/application/kohakutpu/application/kernels/matmul.py:46-64`; `kernels/vector.py:82-86`; evidence: silicon `mm_` shapes: `gm` 3..64, `gn` 8 and 32, `nk` 1 and 2)
- **ISA.MG.081** `silicon` Tiles are dealt round robin over `min(mg.count, tiles)` clusters (tile `u + ncl*i` on cluster u); each tile is `zero`, then per K chunk fill A, fill B, gemm, then one drain into a `tile=(tm/4, tn/4)` f16 scratch the vector cores read. (code: `kernels/matmul.py:118-186`; evidence: `mm_6a1f44575f` alternates `mg[0]`, `mg[1]`)
- **ISA.MG.082** `silicon` A drain to memory becomes the tile's last sweep's `emit` plus a fused DRAIN placed just before the cluster's next emitting sweep (or last); the last sweep's fused drain frees every bank for the next stage. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; evidence: `mm_6a1f44575f`: `drain h1+0 fused n=240` before the next `gemm ... emit=`)
- **ISA.MG.083** `RTL` A drain to a core (`drain c -> vc[H]`) sends the tile's f16 sub-tiles into core H's L1 (one granule a sub-tile), COUNTed; inside a stream the sweep emits under credits and a fused drain collects it. (code: `ktpu-l2/src/lower/cluster.rs`; doc: `software/language/kohakutpu/language/dsl/ops.py:287-288`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_tiles_drain_into_the_cores_and_overlap`, `::test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers`)

## The quantiser step

- **ISA.MG.090** `silicon` Cluster operands in memory are made once per tensor by a mover pass through the transform slot (descriptor mode 5): transform id 1 is the MXFP7 quantiser, `mode[0]` 0 the A packing, 1 the B packing; one entry is 8 source words (4 rows x 32 FP16, 2048 bits) in and 4 MXFP7 words out. A FILL never transforms. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/mover.rs`; `ktpu-isa/src/unit/mover.rs`; RTL `src/kohakutpu/transform/xform_bank.v`, `mx_quant.v`; doc: `docs/spec/transform-slot.md:103-120`; `number-format.md:199-272`; evidence: the card-run tally `fn.quant` 172 in 25 kernels, A and B (`bpack`) packings)
- **ISA.MG.091** `silicon` The quantiser's source is FP16 rows of whole 32-byte words (columns, pitch and offset multiples of 16 values); rows and columns past the tensor are zero (a zero-padded copy first when the extents are not whole groups). (code: `kernels/matmul.py:67-115`; `ktpu-l2/src/lower/mover.rs`; evidence: `quant_` kernels, e.g. `quant_11e4cccd11` (x `f16[6400, 400]` into `mx7a[6400, 416]`))
- **ISA.MG.092** `RTL` The quantiser's output equals `ktpu_l1::layout::tensor::pack_a` / `pack_b` byte for byte. (code: `ktpu-l1/src/layout/tensor.rs`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_mover_quantises_and_tiles`; `tests/sysnode/mx_quant_tb.v` against `numerics/mxfp7.py`)
- **ISA.MG.093** `silicon` The vector core packs MXFP7 itself (`vpack ... mx7`, 8 chunks into one entry, A or B packing); an L2 value made this way feeds a FILL like a mover-made one. (code: `ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs`; RTL `src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:471,516,1022-1034`; evidence: `test_l1_examples.py::test_l2_mlp_quantises_its_activation_on_the_core`; vector `ISA.MG.093.card` on the v9t6 card, `CARD2` in [vector.md](vector.md), its raw y against the MXFP7 packing)
