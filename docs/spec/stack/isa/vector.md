# ISA: the V2 vector core

What the stack relies on in the V2 vector core (`src/kohakutpu/vector2/`, framed
by `src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v`): its state, its 32-bit instruction words,
the CU instructions that load and start it, and the ordering rules between its
engines. Clause format, ids and evidence tags: [../README.md](../README.md).
Number formats (E8M15, MXFP7) are defined in
[../numerics/formats.md](../numerics/formats.md); this page states only where a
conversion happens.

Paths are relative to the repository root. `tally` is the instruction
tally of the card run's kernels; "N in K" means N instructions in K of the
455 kernels the v9t6 card ran. Verilated card tests are in
`software/application/tests/test_l1_examples.py` (`test_l1`), RTL testbenches
in `tests/`.

`CARD2` is the v9t6 card batch of 2026-10-22: node 0 over XDMA at the idle
profile, sys 100 MHz, today's tree, every vector's clause and mutants run in
both stacks (Rust 287/287, Python 232/232; Linux host ledgers
`~/ktpu/sweep/cb-rs.done` and `cb-py.done`). It is the silicon evidence of
ISA.VC.006, .013, .024, .026, .055, .057, .062, .074, .076, .080, .081, .082,
.095, .105, .112, .145 and .165, through their `isa.card.*` vectors.

The v9t6 build: `VEC_CORE v21` (`scripts/tcl/v9t3/00_config.tcl:11`, inherited
by v9t4..v9t6), so `RW` 7 and `RF_COPIES` 2 (`scripts/tcl/v8t3/00_config.tcl:119-120`),
`DUALQ` 0 (no `V2_DUALQ` define), `V2_MQ_SKID` 1 (`scripts/tcl/v9t5/00_config.tcl:23`),
every other parameter at its `v2_core.v` default.

## 1. Machine parameters

- **ISA.VC.001** `silicon` A vector core has `vc.lanes` [16] lanes; one math beat processes one chunk of 16 elements. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:11-15; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:12-14; evidence: every math instruction in tally image, e.g. vmul 7225 in 173)
- **ISA.VC.002** `silicon` A vector register is `vc.chunks` [8] chunks of `vc.lanes` elements, `vc.vlmax` [128] elements, each a 24-bit E8M15 value. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:37,197-198; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:39-41; evidence: tally image vunpk 8581 in 279, vpack 5868 in 279)
- **ISA.VC.003** `silicon` The v9t6 core has `vc.regs` [128] vector registers (2^`RW`, `RW` 7); words naming registers 0..31 run on it. Registers 32.. are ISA.VC.004. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:70-75,197; scripts/tcl/v8t3/00_config.tcl:116-121; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:39,363-365; evidence: silicon kernels name v0..v31 only (grep of the 455 sources), target ktpu.v9)
- **ISA.VC.004** `unproven` Registers 32..`vc.regs`-1 are named through the high register-field bits: past `RW` 5 each register field's high `RW`-5 bits sit above bit 31 of the instruction word, vc at bit 32, then vb, va, vd (a walk's `reg` with vd), carried by IMEM payload bits [39:32]. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:199-200,247-261,279-290; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:230-233; evidence: none; silicon words name v0..v31 and the Verilated cards in `test_l1` are `vlt_card_v9_*_v2`, 32 registers)
- **ISA.VC.005** `silicon` The core has `vc.sregs` [16] scalar registers S0..S15 and `vc.kregs` [4] constant registers K0..K3, 24 bits each. Silicon images read S0, S1, S2, S15 and K0..K2; S3..S14 are ISA.VC.013, K3 ISA.VC.014. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:213-214; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:213; evidence: silicon `vmul v20, s0, v4`, `vsel v13, v4, k0, v0` (kernel 0465d012), `vmul v4, v0, s1` (kernel 02488988), `seti s15 = 469`, tally image seti 320 in 185)
- **ISA.VC.006** `silicon` A predicate register holds `vc.lanes` x `vc.chunks` [128] bits, one per lane per chunk; P1 is written and read as ISA.VC.057, ISA.VC.080-082 state. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:332,399-407; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:122-124; evidence: software/application/tests/api/test_tensor.py::test_op_on_the_card[where] on the `dev` Verilated card: `kp.where(T(X) > T(Y), T(X), 0.0)` lowers through kernels/expr.py:209-219 to L2 `cmpgt` + `sel`, which the `select` pass leaves (its arms are not x and a scale of x), and ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs emits `vcmpgt p1, ..`, `vmov d, k0`, `vmov d, k1 if=p1` over 128-element rows)
- **ISA.VC.007** `silicon` L1 is `vc.l1_words` [512] words of `die.word_bytes` [32] bytes (256 bits), in `vc.l1_banks` [2] banks selected by the word address MSB. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:39,192-194,263-275; ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/vector.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:35-37; evidence: silicon L1 offsets up to l1[510] (kernel 0465d012))
- **ISA.VC.008** `silicon` An L1 word holds fp16 element e of 0..15 at bits [16e+15:16e]; lane e of a chunk takes element e. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:831,981-982; evidence: tally image vunpk fmt f16 8131, vpack f16 5868)
- **ISA.VC.009** `silicon` The instruction queues are `vc.queue_depth` [8] entries for math (MQ), unpack (UQ) and pack (PQ), and `vc.dma_queue_depth` [4] entries for fill (FQ) and drain (DQ). Dispatch stops while the target queue is full. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:45,394-453,505-538; ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/vector.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:26-33; evidence: every silicon image)
- **ISA.VC.010** `silicon` `vc.dualq` [0]: one unpack queue and one pack queue. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:46-52; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:352; evidence: v9t6 build defines no V2_DUALQ)
- **ISA.VC.011** `silicon` The core has `vc.descriptors` [8] address descriptors a0..a7, each a 40-bit base and `vc.descriptor_dims` [4] (stride, bound) dimensions. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_agu.v:1-2,45-47; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:179; evidence: tally image desc 1275 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.012** `silicon` Instruction memory holds `vc.imem_words` [1024] words; a RUN starts below `vc.run_reach` [512]. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:38,193; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; ktpu-l1/src/lower/imem.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:227-228; evidence: every silicon image RUN started below 512)
- **ISA.VC.013** `silicon` S3..S14 hold and supply values as ISA.VC.005 states. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:213; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_softmax (:199) and test_layernorm (:207) (`seti s5 = passes`, `loop s5`); test_l1 test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303) (`getstk s14`, `skipz s14`))
- **ISA.VC.014** `unproven` K3 is read as a K operand (selector 3, reg & 3 = 3) like K0..K2. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:214,1675-1676; evidence: none; no silicon kernel or named test names k3)
- **ISA.VC.015** `unproven` There are `vc.preds` [4] predicate registers P0..P3; P0, P2 and P3 behave as P1 does (ISA.VC.006). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:332,399-407; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/modulo.rs; evidence: none; the L2 lowering names P1, and P2/P3 only when the modulo strategy places copies)

## 2. Core state and its lifetime

- **ISA.VC.020** `silicon` After core reset K0 = 0.0, K1 = 1.0 (0x3F8000), K2 = -1.0 (0xBF8000). The other reset values are ISA.VC.027. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1675-1676; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: silicon images use k0, k1, k2 as constants and never write them (kernels 0465d012, 0bbbc7fc))
- **ISA.VC.021** `silicon` A RUN restarts the DMA running offsets at 0 (ISA.VC.124) and keeps IMEM, so a resident program runs again by a RUN at its slot (ISA.VC.037). The rest of what a RUN clears and keeps is ISA.VC.028. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1683-1686; ktpu-l1/src/lower/imem.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:154-155; evidence: tally image vfill rel 2872, vdrain rel 1891 under `ainc` in 192 kernels; silicon kernel c965164a runs image `expand_9be27f3268_n0_vc0_0` twice (`run` at its lines 88 and 90))
- **ISA.VC.022** `silicon` The configuration persists across RUNs, so an image must not assume the reset values: the encoder enters an image with the configuration unknown and writes every CHKVL, XB, USTR and PSTR value an instruction needs before it. (code: ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: every silicon image was encoded this way)
- **ISA.VC.023** `silicon` The running offsets restart at 0 at each RUN; the encoder writes `ainc aN 0` at image entry for every descriptor a `rel` transfer steps before the image's own `ainc` sets it. That AINC itself persists across RUNs is ISA.VC.028. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1686,1718; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: tally image ainc 612 in 192)
- **ISA.VC.024** `silicon` A program writes a predicate before an instruction reads it: the L2 lowering emits each compare before the predicated move that reads it, in one image. (code: ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:400; evidence: software/application/tests/api/test_tensor.py::test_op_on_the_card[where] (`vcmpgt p1` then `vmov d, k1 if=p1`))
- **ISA.VC.025** `unproven` S registers are not reset; their value after core reset is undefined. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:213,1665-1676; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.026** `silicon` GETSTK clears the sticky bits; a program reads GETSTK once before its first EXP2D to discard what an earlier RUN left. That nothing but core reset and GETSTK clears them is ISA.VC.028. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:408-412; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1723-1726; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303), both scales)
- **ISA.VC.027** `unproven` After core reset: VL = `vc.vlmax`, CHK = 0x1111 (every operand base 0, stride 1), XB = 0, UPTR = UINC = PPTR = PINC = 0, USTR = PSTR = (s0 1, s1 4), every AINC 0, K3 = 0. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1665-1676; evidence: none; the encoder writes every one of them before use, ISA.VC.022)
- **ISA.VC.028** `unproven` A RUN clears every queue and engine, the per-kind instruction counts and the marks. It keeps the vector registers, L1, descriptors, S, K, VL, CHK, XB, the walk pointers and strides, every AINC, the predicates, the sticky bits (cleared only by core reset and GETSTK) and the RX and CR counters (ISA.VC.152). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:205-207,1470-1506,1683-1691; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:408-412; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:154-155; evidence: none; every silicon image and named test rewrites the state it reads)
- **ISA.VC.029** `unproven` The predicates are not reset. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:400; evidence: none)

## 3. CU instructions: IMEM, DESC, RUN

- **ISA.VC.030** `silicon` A core takes three CU instructions, each a 256-bit `CU_INST` payload with op [255:252]: 1 IMEM, 2 DESC, 3 RUN. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:7-11,174-181,540-562; evidence: tally image desc 1275 in 290, `<image>` 381 RUNs in 290)
- **ISA.VC.031** `silicon` IMEM writes word [31:0] at instruction address {[242], [251:243]}. Payload bits [39:32] for a core of more than 32 registers are ISA.VC.004. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:543-549; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:247-261; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:227-233; evidence: every silicon image loaded through IMEM below word 512)
- **ISA.VC.032** `silicon` A word encoded for 32 registers has the high register-field bits above bit 31 (ISA.VC.004) zero and runs unchanged on a core of `RW` 7 (128 registers). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:199-200,279-290; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:230-233; evidence: the 32-register images of the silicon run executed on the v9t6 `RW` 7 core)
- **ISA.VC.033** `unproven` IMEM words 512..1023 (address bit [242] set) load and execute. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:249-250; evidence: none naming an image placed above 512)
- **ISA.VC.034** `silicon` DESC writes field `fld` [248:246] of descriptor `ad` [251:249]: field 0 the base, a 40-bit address split as value [245:212] (low 34) and value_hi [68:63] (high 6); fields 1..4 dimensions 0..3, value [33:16] a signed 18-bit byte stride and [15:0] a 16-bit bound. Fields 5..7 are ISA.VC.192. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:180,551-555; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_agu.v:49,61-68; evidence: tally image desc 1275 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.035** `silicon` RUN starts the loaded program at pc [251:243] (9 bits) and retires when the program reaches VHALT; its completion carries the RUN's busy-cycle count (32 bits). (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:557-573; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1678,1743-1745; evidence: tally image halt 381 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.036** `silicon` The CU takes the next CU instruction only while no RUN is active, so IMEM and DESC never change under a running program. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:539-573; ktpu-l1/src/lower/imem.rs; evidence: silicon packages send IMEM/DESC/RUN groups back to back to one core)
- **ISA.VC.037** `silicon` VLOOP and VSKIPZ are pc-relative, so a program runs at any base; the stack keeps several programs resident and sends a resident program as its DESC words and a RUN at its slot. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:546-548,1740-1741; ktpu-l1/src/lower/imem.rs; evidence: tally image loop 160 in 137)
- **ISA.VC.038** `unproven` A RUN that faults retires as SIG_FAULT carrying the fault code (ISA.VC.190). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:568-573; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1692-1694; evidence: none; no silicon RUN faulted)
- **ISA.VC.039** `unproven` A CU instruction with any other op retires with no effect. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:561; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.192** `unproven` A DESC to field 5, 6 or 7 is ignored. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_agu.v:49,61-68; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:551-555; evidence: none; the encoder writes fields 0..4 only)

## 4. Instruction words

- **ISA.VC.040** `silicon` An instruction is a 32-bit word with opcode [31:27]: 0x00..0x12 math (which of them ran: ISA.VC.054-057), 0x13 VCFG, 0x14 VUNPK, 0x15 VPACK, 0x16 VSYNC, 0x1A VSETI, 0x1B VLOOP, 0x1C VBAR, 0x1D VFILL, 0x1E VDRAIN, 0x1F VHALT. The other opcodes are ISA.VC.044 and ISA.VC.045. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:167-171,316,505-541; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:207-225; evidence: tally image `vl=` and `xb` modifiers (VCFG words), vunpk 8581, vpack 5868, vsync 7327, seti 320, loop 160, vbar 395, vfill 3076, vdrain 1891, halt 381)
- **ISA.VC.041** `silicon` Field layouts, MSB first. Math: vd [26:22], va [21:17], vb [16:12], vc [11:7], sa [6:5], sb [4:3], sc [2:1], om [0]. VCFG: sel [26:23], payload [22:0]. VUNPK/VPACK: reg [26:22], mode [21:20], n-1 [19:17], cb [16:14], rel [13], q [12], off [11:0]. VSYNC: waiter [26:24], on [23:21], slack [20:15], marked [14], mark [13:11], q [10]. VSETI: sreg [25:22], to_k [0]. VLOOP: sreg [20:17], body [16:7]. VBAR: q [10]. VFILL: rel [26], ad [23:21], off [13:0]. VDRAIN: rel [26], signal [25], node [24], ad [23:21], dst_x [20:17], dst_y [16:13], buf_id [12:9], off [8:0]. VHALT: no fields. VWAIT, VMARK, VPOST, VSKIPZ: ISA.VC.044; VSETVL: ISA.VC.045. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:278-314,1221-1229,1286-1294; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:211-225; evidence: tally image, every instruction listed here)
- **ISA.VC.042** `silicon` Bits a format leaves unused are encoded zero. The decoder's rejection of a nonzero unused bit is ISA.VC.046. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: every silicon word came from these formats)
- **ISA.VC.043** `unproven` A value too wide for its field is refused at encode time (`ISAError`), never truncated into the neighbouring field. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; ktpu-isa/src/layout/format.rs; evidence: none; a refusal is a software path no silicon image or named card test takes)
- **ISA.VC.044** `RTL` 0x16 is also VMARK (waiter 5) and VWAIT (on = 7), 0x17 VSKIPZ, 0x19 VPOST. Layouts: VWAIT n [20:11], q [10], counter [9]; VMARK as VSYNC with marked 0; VPOST dst_x [26:23], dst_y [22:19], n [15:0]; VSKIPZ sreg [25:22], skip [7:0]. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:278-314,519-537; evidence: test_l1 test_softmax (:199) (`vmark`), test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244) (`vwait drain rx`), test_l2_tiles_drain_into_the_cores_and_overlap (:340) (`vpost`, `vwait unpack rx`), test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303) (`skipz`))
- **ISA.VC.045** `unproven` 0x18 is VSETVL, sreg [20:17]; every 5-bit opcode decodes (the front end's default F_OPCODE arm is unreachable). (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:509-539; evidence: none; nothing emits `vsetvl`)
- **ISA.VC.046** `unproven` The decoder (`isa.core.CORE`) rejects a word with a nonzero unused bit. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: none; an offline decode, not a card or RTL run)

## 5. Math instructions

- **ISA.VC.050** `silicon` A math word names destination vd and sources a (va, sa), b (vb, sb), c (vc, sc); a selector is 0 V (vector register), 1 S (S[reg & 15]), 3 K (K[reg & 3]). (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:173,333-340; evidence: silicon `vmul v4, k2, v19`, `vmul v20, s0, v4`, `vmax v4, v10, s2`)
- **ISA.VC.051** `unproven` Selector 2 on any of sa, sb, sc faults F_SRC, whether or not the op reads that operand. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:506; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.052** `silicon` An S or K operand's value is read at dispatch and applied to every lane and beat; a later VSETI does not change an op already dispatched. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1641-1642; evidence: silicon `seti s0 = ...` followed by `vmul vD, s0, vX` (kernel 0465d012))
- **ISA.VC.053** `silicon` Operand slots each op reads, and the L1 text form `op vd, a[, b][, c]`: unary vmov vexp2 vlog2 vinv vrsqrt read a; vadd reads a, c; vmul vmax read a, b; vsel reads a, b, c. An unread operand raises no hazard. The other ops' slots are ISA.VC.063 and ISA.VC.064. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:320-331; software/language/kohakutpu/language/l1/ops.py:6-25; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: silicon `vadd v8, v10, v12` (a, c), `vsel v13, v4, k0, v0` (a, b, c), `vmul v20, s0, v4`, `vinv`, `vexp2`, `vrsqrt`, `vlog2` (tally image))
- **ISA.VC.054** `silicon` Opcodes and results (E8M15): vmov 0x00 d = a; vadd 0x03 d = a + c; vmul 0x05 d = a x b; vmax 0x08 d = b if a < b else a; vsel 0x0A d = a if c is nonzero (c bits [22:0] not all zero) else b; vexp2 0x0E d = 2^a; vlog2 0x0F d = log2 a; vinv 0x10 d = 1/a; vrsqrt 0x11 d = 1/sqrt a. The core maps opcodes 0x0E..0x11 to ALU ops 16..19 and 0x12 to 20 before the ALU. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:318-319; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_alu.v:101-108,219-271; evidence: tally image vmul 7225 in 173, vadd 4441 in 178, vmov 1799 in 49, vsel 1272 in 47, vinv 664 in 35, vexp2 378 in 14, vmax 343 in 16, vrsqrt 131 in 23, vlog2 8 in 1)
- **ISA.VC.055** `silicon` vsub 0x04 d = a - c; vfma 0x06 d = a x b + c; vfnma 0x07 d = c - a x b; vexp2d 0x12 d = 2^(a - floor(b)). The clamp of b is ISA.VC.065. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_alu.v:5-9,239-271; evidence: test_l1 test_layernorm (:207) for vsub vfma vfnma, test_softmax (:199) for vexp2d)
- **ISA.VC.056** `unproven` vneg 0x01 flips the sign of a; vabs 0x02 clears it; vmin 0x09 d = b if a > b else a. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_alu.v:224-237; evidence: none; tests/vector/vec_alu_tb.v drives `vec_alu`, a separate module from the core's `v2_alu` (instantiated at src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:316), and no named card test was verified to emit these ops)
- **ISA.VC.057** `silicon` vcmpgt 0x0C compares a with b and writes the result bit into predicate P[pr] (ISA.VC.080); it writes no vector register. vcmplt, vcmpeq and the NaN and signed-zero cases are ISA.VC.066. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:225-229,1657,1686,1731-1733; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:192-193; evidence: tally image vfill rel 2872, vdrain rel 1891)

- **ISA.VC.126** `silicon` VDRAIN ad, off without `node` writes L1 words (off + i) mod `vc.l1_words` to the walk's memory addresses. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1286-1336; evidence: tally image vdrain 1891 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.127** `unproven` A VFILL or VDRAIN walk of more than `vc.walk_max` [256] words faults F_LEN. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1247-1248,1301-1302; ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/vector.rs; evidence: none for the fault; silicon walks are at most 64 words)
- **ISA.VC.128** `silicon` A VFILL is finished when every word has landed in L1; a VDRAIN is finished when it has read its last L1 word and handed it to the write port, not when memory holds it. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1188-1208,1346-1347; evidence: silicon `vsync pack on=drain` before reusing a drained L1 range, 1891 drains)
- **ISA.VC.129** `silicon` Fill reads go out as runs of at most 255 words over a contiguous dimension 0 (stride 32), never crossing a 256-word L1 boundary; drain writes go out as bursts of at most 8 words over a contiguous dimension 0, never crossing a 256-byte boundary. Other walks go a word at a time. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1131-1139,1143-1166; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:183-190; evidence: every silicon fill and drain)
- **ISA.VC.130** `RTL` VDRAIN with `node` set is a peer drain: its words go as one CU_DATA burst to the unit at (dst_x, dst_y), buffer buf_id, read from L1 words off.. consecutively; the descriptor gives only the count (product of bounds) and the base, whose fields are the destination L1 granule [15:0], the ack unit {y, x} [23:16], the far mesh [25:24], the far unit {y, x} [33:26] and COUNT [34]. `rel` adds the running offset to the whole base value. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1286-1314; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:224; evidence: test_l1 test_push_and_recv (:119), test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244))
- **ISA.VC.131** `RTL` A peer drain to another mesh names this mesh's system port in (dst_x, dst_y) and the far mesh and unit in the base; ack {y, x} = 0 sends SIG_DATA_RECEIVED (when `signal`) to the sender, which a sending core drops. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/mod.rs; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:295-300; evidence: test_l1 test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244))
- **ISA.VC.132** `RTL` A peer drain with COUNT set flags its burst so the receiver counts its granules (RX, ISA.VC.152). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1309; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:165-169; evidence: test_l1 test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244))
- **ISA.VC.133** `unproven` After core reset each descriptor is base 0, strides 0, bounds 1; descriptors persist across RUNs. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_agu.v:52-60; evidence: none; every silicon RUN and named test sends its descriptors before the RUN, ISA.VC.037)

## 10. Engines and ordering

- **ISA.VC.140** `silicon` The front end dispatches in program order, one instruction a cycle, into five engines: FILL (VFILL), DRAIN (VDRAIN, VPOST), UNPACK (VUNPK, VBAR), MATH (math), PACK (VPACK); VSYNC and VWAIT ride the queue of their waiter. Each engine runs its queue in order. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1-31,1632-1637; ktpu-verify/src/sim/units/vector.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:21-33; evidence: every silicon image)
- **ISA.VC.141** `silicon` Register hazards are the hardware's: math, unpack and pack wait for every older access to the registers they read or write (RAW, WAR, WAW) and dispatch never stalls on them. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:16-24,342-364,1539-1543; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:52-78; evidence: silicon `rows` kernels unpack into, compute on and pack from the same registers with no VSYNC between)
- **ISA.VC.142** `silicon` L1 hazards are the program's: nothing orders an engine's L1 access against another engine's except VSYNC, marks, VBAR and VWAIT. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:26-28; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:89-90; evidence: tally image vsync 7327 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.143** `silicon` VSYNC waiter: 0 the front end (dispatch stops), 1 the unpack queue, 3 the pack queue, 4 the fill queue, 6 the drain queue. Kind (`on`): 0 VFILL, 1 VUNPK queue 0, 3 VPACK queue 0, 4 VDRAIN. Waiter 5, kinds 2 and 7: ISA.VC.155; waiter 2, kinds 5 and 6: ISA.VC.156. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:176-185; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:132-140; evidence: silicon waiters fe, fill, unpack, pack, drain and kinds fill, unpack, pack, drain (tally image vsync flags))
- **ISA.VC.144** `silicon` VSYNC waiter, on, slack makes the waiter wait until every `on` instruction dispatched before the VSYNC has finished, except the youngest `slack` (0..63) of them. A queue waiter's sync rides that queue, so only that engine waits. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:455-463,475,488-490,1628; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:126-146; evidence: tally image vsync slack 0..63)
- **ISA.VC.145** `silicon` A sync whose kind is math orders as ISA.VC.144 states. A math-queue waiter is ISA.VC.157. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:594,630-632; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303) (`vsync fe on=math` before `getstk`); no silicon kernel used kind math, and test_softmax / test_layernorm sync on fill, unpack, pack, drain and marks only)
- **ISA.VC.146** `RTL` VMARK m, on, slack (VSYNC waiter 5) records mark m (0..7) = kind `on` and the count dispatched so far less `slack`; a later VSYNC with `marked` and mark m waits for exactly those instructions, whatever has been dispatched since. Marks restart at each RUN. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:237-240,1470-1479; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_softmax (:199), test_layernorm (:207))
- **ISA.VC.147** `unproven` A VSYNC waiter above 6, or a mark (waiter 5) of kind 7, faults F_SYNC. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:519; evidence: none for the fault)
- **ISA.VC.148** `silicon` VBAR q makes the unpack queue wait for every VFILL dispatched before it. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:529,1650; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: tally image vbar 395 in 192)
- **ISA.VC.149** `unproven` On a `vc.dualq` 0 core the q bit of VUNPK, VPACK, VSYNC, VWAIT and VBAR is ignored (queue 0), and kinds 5 / 6 count as kinds 1 / 3. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:46-47,301-302,414-415; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:148-150; evidence: none; every silicon word has q = 0)
- **ISA.VC.150** `unproven` On a `vc.dualq` 1 core q selects the second unpack or pack queue, and the engine starts queue 0's head when ready, else queue 1's. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:411-449,742; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:92-94; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.151** `RTL` VWAIT waiter, counter, n (VSYNC with on = 7) raises its counter's target by n (10 bits) at dispatch and makes the waiter wait until the counter reaches the target: counter 0 RX counts 32-byte granules landed in L1 by CU_DATA bursts flagged COUNT, counter 1 CR counts credits received in CU_SIGNAL CREDITs. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:306-309,475,1492-1506; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:152-163; evidence: test_l1 test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244) for RX; test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers (:362) for CR)
- **ISA.VC.152** `RTL` The RX and CR counters and targets are reset only with the core and persist across RUNs, so data and credits may arrive before the RUN that waits for them. A landed granule counts the cycle after its L1 write, so a reader the count releases sees it. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1492-1506; evidence: test_l1 test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244))
- **ISA.VC.153** `RTL` VPOST dst, n sends a CU_SIGNAL CREDIT of n (16 bits) to the unit at dst from the drain queue: after every VDRAIN dispatched before it, and after a drain-queue VSYNC before it. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1179-1181,1221-1229; ktpu-l2/src/lower/mod.rs; doc: docs/projects/kohakutpu/vector-core-v2.md:165-169; evidence: test_l1 test_l2_tiles_drain_into_the_cores_and_overlap (:340))
- **ISA.VC.154** `silicon` VHALT waits until every queue is empty and every engine idle (fills landed, drains handed to the port, posts sent), then halts the RUN. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:538,1349-1352,1743-1745; evidence: tally image halt 381 in 290)
- **ISA.VC.155** `RTL` VSYNC waiter 5 records a mark (VMARK); kind 2 is math, kind 7 a count (VWAIT). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:176-185; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_softmax (:199) (`vmark m0 on=fill`); test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303) (`on=math`); test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244) (`vwait drain rx`))
- **ISA.VC.156** `unproven` VSYNC waiter 2 is the math queue; kinds 5 and 6 are VUNPK and VPACK queue 1. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:176-185; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: none; no silicon kernel or named test names a math waiter or queue 1)
- **ISA.VC.157** `unproven` A sync whose waiter is the math queue rides that queue and orders as ISA.VC.144 states. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:523,594,630-632; evidence: none; nothing emits `vsync math`)

## 11. Front-end control

- **ISA.VC.160** `silicon` VSETI sreg (two words): the word after it is a 24-bit immediate written to S[sreg], or to K3 with to_k; the immediate word is not executed. The encoder writes a float constant as E8M15 and an integer as its low 24 bits. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:534,1703,1735-1736; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: silicon `seti s0 = 0.008333333767950535`, `seti s15 = 469`)
- **ISA.VC.161** `unproven` VSETI to K3 sets K3, which persists until core reset. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1675-1676,1735; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.162** `silicon` VLOOP sreg, body: the next `body` words run S[sreg] times (S read as a raw 24-bit integer at dispatch), with no loop overhead. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:551-555,1737-1742; evidence: silicon `seti s15 = 469` / `loop s15`, tally image loop 160 in 137)
- **ISA.VC.163** `unproven` A VLOOP dispatched while a loop is active faults F_LOOP: loops do not nest. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:535; ktpu-l1/src/lower/image/mod.rs; evidence: none for the fault)
- **ISA.VC.164** `unproven` VLOOP with S[sreg] = 0 runs the body once; the stack does not rely on it (Conflicts, item 4). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:553-554; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.165** `silicon` VSKIPZ sreg, skip: when S[sreg] (raw 24 bits) is zero the next `skip` words are skipped, counted in words (a VSETI is two). (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:494,546-548; evidence: test_l1 test_l2_sticky_softmax_on_both_paths (:303))
- **ISA.VC.166** `unproven` VSETVL sreg sets VL = S[sreg]; S[sreg] (raw 24 bits) of 0 or above `vc.vlmax` faults F_VL. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:493,531-532,1734; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.167** `silicon` CHKVL takes VL - 1 in 7 bits, so it cannot express VL 0 or VL above 128 and does not fault (its F_VL arm needs VL - 1 >= 128). The encoder's refusal is ISA.VC.168. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:510-512,1719-1722; evidence: tally image vl=64 flags)
- **ISA.VC.168** `unproven` The encoder refuses a VL outside 1..`vc.vlmax`. (code: ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: none; a software refusal no card or RTL run takes)

## 12. CU_DATA into the core, and the landing pool

- **ISA.VC.180** `RTL` A CU_DATA burst of n words at offset `off` lands in L1 words off..off+n-1 when buf_id is 0 and off + n - 1 < `vc.l1_words`. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:291-306; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:1056-1057; evidence: test_l1 test_push_and_recv (:119))
- **ISA.VC.181** `unproven` Any other burst is dropped, and so is the rest of a burst into which a second sender's flit arrives; either latches F_CUDATA, which faults the running RUN or the next one. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:330-336,383-385,394-402; src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:503-504,1687-1690,1750; evidence: none)
- **ISA.VC.182** `RTL` A burst's flags[0] has the core send SIG_DATA_RECEIVED {24'd0, buf_id} to the ack unit (or the sender when ack is 0) once the last word lands; flags[1] COUNT counts its granules into RX. (code: src/kohakutpu/vector/vec_cu.v:300,386-387,406-408; doc: docs/spec/flit-format.md:356-363; evidence: test_l1 test_push_and_recv (:119), test_vector_peer_drain_across_meshes (:244))
- **ISA.VC.183** `RTL` The stack lands cluster-to-core tiles in the top 256 words (the stack's landing pool) of a receiving core's L1, back to back, wrapping to the pool start when the next tile would pass the L1 end; two consecutive tiles of a stream fit in the pool, and the core's own buffers sit below it. The core waits for a tile with `vwait unpack rx n=WORDS` and returns its slot with VPOST once its unpacks have read it. (code: ktpu-l2/src/lower/mod.rs; ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/mod.rs; evidence: test_l1 test_l2_tiles_drain_into_the_cores_and_overlap (:340), test_l2_mlp_with_nothing_in_memory_between_layers (:362))

## 13. Faults

- **ISA.VC.190** `unproven` A fault stops dispatch at once and retires the RUN as SIG_FAULT with the code; engines do not drain. The codes a V2 core raises: 5 F_LEN, 6 F_LOOP, 7 F_VL, 9 F_CUDATA, 10 F_GT4, 11 F_SRC, 12 F_MODE, 13 F_SYNC. Codes 1 F_DTYPE, 2 F_VSRC, 3 F_CHAIN and 8 F_REDVL belong to the V1 core; 4 F_OPCODE is declared and unreachable (every opcode decodes). (code: src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:187-189,497-543,1692-1694; ktpu-isa/src/unit/vector/core.rs; evidence: none naming a V2 fault)
- **ISA.VC.191** `unproven` After a fault the stack assumes nothing about the core's resident programs and descriptors. (code: ktpu-l1/src/lower/imem.rs; evidence: none; no silicon RUN faulted)

## 14. Scheduling-model items (no observable ISA effect)

These disagree between the stack's timing models and do not change what an
instruction does; they are listed so a timing ruling can cite them.

- Unpack-to-math latency: 12 in `ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/modulo.rs` and `ktpu-l1/src/sched/timing.rs`, 0 in `ktpu-l1/src/sched/schedule.rs`.
- Unpack/pack occupancy: words moved (F32 x2, MX7 pack 16) in `ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/modulo.rs`; a flat 8 in `ktpu-l1/src/sched/schedule.rs`.
- Pack read latency: 20 in `ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/modulo.rs`; none in `ktpu-l1/src/sched/schedule.rs`.
- Config-word cost: 1 front-end cycle in `ktpu-l1/src/sched/timing.rs`; 4 cycles a change in `ktpu-l1/src/sched/schedule.rs`.
- Crossbar latency +7 (`ktpu-l2/src/lower/vector/modulo.rs`).
- `vc.rf_copies` [2] and `vc.regs` [128] on v9t6: the card runtime compiles with the machine file's values (`application/runtime/card.py` `build_facts`). Read copies change bank-conflict stalls only. `vc.rf_banks` [2] is the register file's bank count, a chunk's bank being its index mod 2 (`src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_lanes.v:3-4,176`).
- `V2_MQ_SKID` 1 on v9t6: the math queue reads full at QD - 2 entries plus a two-entry skid (`src/kohakutpu/vector2/v2_core.v:83-91,396-410`).
